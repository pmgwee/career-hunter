## The two stories that carry the most weight

### 1. "Business requirements come before technical specification"
The lesson from iFAST: sensitive customer records carry **handling rules before they carry
business value**, and a specification is only as good as the domain knowledge behind it.
This is the single most senior-sounding sentence in the archive. Use it in any regulated,
enterprise, fintech or consulting context.

### 2. "Documentation is a starting reference, not proof the system works"
The lesson from the Second Brain build: lifecycle hooks **failed silently** when implemented
from official documentation alone, and context could appear delivered without being complete
or relevant. The response was to instrument every touchpoint, define structured benchmarks
across time windows, and build a live console for hook delivery, retrieval quality, token
usage and deployment drift.

This is the best story on the profile because it contains a **real failure**, a **systematic
response**, and a **generalizable principle**. Use it for any "tell me about a hard technical
problem" or "how do you know your AI system works" question.
